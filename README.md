# illustrated-gospel
https://illustratedgospel.faith/

The Gospel in Illustrations - The Life of Jesus Christ in Word and Image

This project was conceived and completed within 24 hours over Christmas break. Being the season, I was reading the book of Mark and had the idea of creating a standalone publication of individual bible books with illustrations. I've long appreciated illustrated bibles, no doubt because my early and transformative exposure to the gospel was in that form. My unique take was that publishing a single book - say, Mark - could be a more accessible form for readers, young in particular. After all, one short book is less intimidating than a large book of books!

Realizing my lack of artistic talent, I thought I might give AI prompted image generation a try. Without intending to take it very far straight away, I experimented first with Grok but the images were unsatisfactory. Then I asked Claude Sonnet 4.5 to generate an image of John the Baptist for me and was pleasantly surprised by the playful, primitive html / canvas-based image it produced. So I kept going with different scenes and before I knew it I had a fairly complete gospel narrative!

The final act was to stitch all the images together with companion scriptural quotes. I knew from previous research that the Berean Standard Bible (BSB) was a solid, royalty free option available in the public domain (in fact, the version of Mark I was reading was printed out from BSB PDFs available online). Claude did a fine job producing the final, static HTML artifact per my direction.

The finished product is not what I originally conceived - a complete, single book of the gospel with accompanying images - but I think it is a worthy composite narrative of the life of Jesus and I will be happy with any reach it may achieve, big or small, to God's glory.

Blessings to all, Merry Christmas 2025!
